# Code of Conduct

Be straightforward and respectful. That's the whole policy.

In practice that means:

- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, PRs, or anywhere else
  connected to the project.
- Assume good faith; most friction is a misunderstanding.

I'll remove comments, close threads, or block people who don't follow this. Since
the project is hosted on GitHub, its
[Terms of Service](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-of-service)
and [Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines)
apply too.

To report something privately, email:

    scone-void-baggage (at) duck (dot) com

(Replace `(at)` with `@` and `(dot)` with `.`, and remove the spaces.)
